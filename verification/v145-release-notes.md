## Pipo2doo v1.4.5 · build 156

- New **Celebration first** love-day settings: live next-milestone card, compact Celebrate / Reminders / Look & feel tabs, custom milestones, personal note and celebration preview.
- Compact Us & settings with larger avatars, grouped settings, equally sized connection actions, and a slimmer Period & Care profile switcher.
- Our Locket stays below the Today profiles and timezones. Smaller Together progress strip and a compact account-backup panel with **Restore latest** confirmation.
- **Cancel** beside Poke: cancel one occurrence or stop its series, with an optional reason. Cancelled tasks remain in Progress → History without becoming overdue or earning completion credit. Completed history is retained.
- Startup loads independent cloud reads concurrently and pages older appreciation records in the background.

Solo and paired modes use the same updated components. Existing task/list editing permissions and assignee-based notification rules are retained.

### Update

Download **Pipo2doo-v1.4.5.apk** and install over the existing app. Do not uninstall; Android may ask you to confirm the update. The `.apk.sha256` file is an optional integrity checksum.

Both partners should update to see cancellation labels. Older versions display cancelled tasks as done.
