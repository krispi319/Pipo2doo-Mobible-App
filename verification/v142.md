## Scope and limitations

Future edits change only title, assignee, list and tags. Schedule and personal fields
must use Only this occurrence; the editor rejects mixed future/schedule edits rather
than partially applying them. Unrelated tasks with matching titles are not a series.
Individual completion/history and existing occurrence IDs are retained. Daily series
metadata may change to preserve generation. Ordinary weekly occurrence edits may
materialize the next weekly task early to preserve its original details.

The new batch operation saves at most 500 task/list changes per transaction. A larger
edit fails visibly without saving a partial batch. Sync still uses foreground polling;
the status banner reports server state, not partner-device receipt.

No signed APK or release publication was created. Physical two-phone validation and
notification delivery remain outstanding. No Undo/trash feature was added.
